02 / Learn

Images are resources

HTML can place an image in a page with an <img> element. Its src points to an image file; its alt gives a useful text alternative when the image cannot be seen or loaded.

<img src="assets/garden.svg" alt="A small garden with a tree and flowers">

A relative path such as assets/garden.svg starts from the folder containing the web page. Keep the image in that folder structure when moving or downloading your site. Describe the image's purpose, not just its appearance; use empty alternative text when an image is purely decorative.

Plan before coding

Sketch or describe a page with a title, short introduction, image, and a caption. Decide what the image contributes and write its alternative text before adding it.

04 / Reflect

Check your learning

Reveal the ideas

The src attribute points to the file. Useful alt text communicates the image's meaning or purpose to someone who cannot see it; decorative images can use an empty value.

05 / Take it further

Homework
